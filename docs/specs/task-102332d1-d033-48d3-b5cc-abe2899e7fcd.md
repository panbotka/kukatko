Found during the /prerelease walkthrough of the v0.20.0..f704cd5 range (the family-tree epic). Severity: NIT — nothing is wrong in the data, only in how `ctl` counts it.

## What is wrong

`kukatko ctl family relations <subject-uid>` treats a **lone-parent family** as a partnership:
it prints a `partner` row reading `- (no partner recorded)`, and the summary line then
over-counts, e.g. "2 partners · 1 child" for a person who has exactly one partner.

A lone-parent family is created whenever a child is recorded before a partner is: the family row
gets `kind = 'partnership'` with `partner_b_uid = NULL`. That is correct storage — the family is
the node, and the child has to hang off something — but it is not a partner and should not be
presented or counted as one.

## Reproduce (against the box staging context)

    bin/kukatko ctl --context boxstage family add <A> child --name "Někdo Nový"
    bin/kukatko ctl --context boxstage family add <A> partner <B>
    bin/kukatko ctl --context boxstage family relations <A>

Observed on staging for `su79q81hbeponrdq05ln6c4vv9`:

    partner  - (no partner recorded)                          -  partnership  fm1aqff0ch1ggj90s96orbleuo  -
    partner  Testovací osoba 6 (sugmar95oa94kcr04bgbs1m5de)   -  partnership  fmvmk7gcqmo86fv7t4n6qkdslv  5

    2 parents · 1 sibling · 2 partners · 1 child        ← "2 partners", there is one

`GET /subjects/{uid}/relations` returns the same shape (`partners[]` carries an entry whose
`partner` is `null`), so decide deliberately whether this is fixed in `internal/ctl` presentation
only, or whether the API's `partners` list should exclude partner-less families. The **UI is not
affected** — the FamilyStrip's Partner row correctly showed only the one real partner, so a
change to the payload must not regress that.

## Suggested shape

In `internal/ctl`, skip a partner row whose `partner` is null and count only real partners in the
summary — or, if the row is worth keeping so the family uid is visible, label it as a lone-parent
family rather than a partner and leave it out of the count. Cover it with a ctl unit test that
builds a person with one lone-parent family plus one real partnership and asserts the summary
says one partner.

## Out of scope

Do not change how lone-parent families are stored or created — that behaviour is correct and is
what makes half-siblings come out right (see `internal/family` and commit d709a04).
