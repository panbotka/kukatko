# Library grid polish (desktop)

Two visual issues in the photo grid, seen on the box staging (commit 68e805e, 2026-09-06) in a browser that reports a real fine pointer (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` both true). Note that the shared headless browser on the Pi reports `hover: none`, so these can only be judged in a browser with a genuine pointer — see the memory note "headless has no hover" for how to launch one; the phone branch must stay exactly as it is.

## 1. A heart on every tile

At tile density 10 the library paints the favourite heart disc on all 108 visible tiles at once; each disc covers a good part of a ~100 px tile and the wall of hearts competes with the photographs. Screenshot: `/mnt/nas-botka/kukatko-stage-test/18-library-desktop.png`. The control is `web/src/components/library/FavoriteButton.tsx` placed by `PhotoTile.tsx`; the touch rules live in `web/src/styles/tokens.css` (≈ lines 824 and 931, `@media (hover: none), (pointer: coarse)`), and the checkmark already has hover-only behaviour on pointer screens that the heart can follow.

### Requirements

- On hover-capable, fine-pointer screens the heart of a **non-favourite** tile appears only while the tile is hovered or holds keyboard focus (focus-within). A **favourite** tile keeps its filled heart visible at all times so the state stays readable in the grid.
- Selection mode (`kk-tile--checks`) and touch/coarse-pointer screens are unchanged: always-on hearts there are deliberate (see commit 190dfd8, "a phone gets three photographs across, not six under hearts").
- Keyboard users must still be able to reach the heart on every tile (focus reveals it); nothing may become impossible to activate.
- Tests: Vitest with `matchMedia` mocked for both branches (jsdom gotchas: `ontouchstart` is always true — see `web/src/styles/tokens.test.ts` and `tapTargets.test.ts` for the existing prelude conventions).

## 2. Timeline rail on a grid that does not scroll

A 9-photo private album fits in one row, yet the full-height timeline rail with 2013 · 2015 · 2022 · 2024 · 2026 and the "zář 2013" chip sits at the right edge, suggesting scrollable content that is not there. Screenshot: `24-album-private.png` in the same folder. Component: `web/src/components/library/TimelineScrubber.tsx` with `timelineRail.ts`.

### Requirements

- The scrubber is hidden whenever the grid's scroll container has nothing to scroll (content height ≤ viewport), and reappears when it does; re-evaluate on resize, on density change and when the photo count changes (filters, infinite scroll).
- Applies to every page that mounts the scrubber (library, album, label, person, saved search, trash), on desktop and phone.
- Tests: Vitest for the hidden/shown decision on both sides of the threshold and on resize.

## Out of scope

- No change to the timeline's grouping or navigation behaviour.
