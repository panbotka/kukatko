# The phone timeline rail's own tap targets are 16 px and 5 px tall

The app's primary time-navigation control on a phone packs 93 buttons into a 40 px strip, most of them far below any usable tap size.

## Evidence (observed on production, 2026-08-12, 390x844 with a genuinely coarse pointer active)

- 31 year ticks at **39.8 x 16.2 px**, separated by 4.0 px.
- 62 month ticks at **16.0 x 5.2 px**.
- WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 (AA) asks for 24 x 24. The spacing exception does not rescue this: at a 20.2 px pitch, 24 px circles centred on adjacent ticks intersect.
- A fingertip is roughly 34-45 CSS px.

Source: `web/src/components/library/TimelineScrubber.tsx` plus `web/src/styles/app.css:1108-1169`.

Missing by one tick is a jump of tens of thousands of pixels with no undo except scrolling back — and the library has 20 906 photos spanning decades, so a stray hit is expensive.

## Requirements

- On a coarse pointer, the rail's interactive targets meet the app's own 44 px floor, or the 24 px AA floor at minimum, without the rail growing wide enough to cover the grid.
- The month ticks stop being 62 five-pixel buttons. If they are useful as visual texture, they become decorative — `aria-hidden`, not focusable, not tappable — and the year ticks carry the interaction.
- The rail remains usable with a fine pointer (mouse) at its current precision.
- Keyboard and screen-reader access to the rail is no worse than today.
- A test pins the target sizes under a coarse pointer so this cannot silently regress.

## Implementation Notes

Measuring this requires forcing `pointer: coarse`, which `agent-browser set device` does **not** do on its own. Hold a persistent CDP websocket to the page target and re-apply `Emulation.setEmulatedMedia` with `pointer=coarse` plus `Emulation.setTouchEmulationEnabled` after every navigation — the overrides are per-client and revert when the client disconnects. Confirm with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)').matches` in the same evaluation as the measurement, or the numbers are desktop numbers.

## Constraints

- **No mutable operations against production data.** Read-only browsing of production is allowed for verification; writes of any kind are not.
- Related but separate task: the rail covering the Filters button. These two touch the same component — do not undo that fix.
- `make check` must pass. `make check-box` runs the same gate on the build box and is much faster.
