# Albums are told apart only by their name, because the covers repeat

On the first screen of `/albums`, **four** albums had exactly the same cover (a scan of a
photo book title page) and another **two** shared a picture of a fire. On a phone, where two
albums fit per row, that is two screens of identical tiles. The cover is apparently taken as
"the newest photo in the album", so overlapping albums look the same.

A grid of cards only makes sense when the cards differ. When they do not, it is worse than a
list: it takes more space and helps less.

## Requirements
- Pick the cover so it does not repeat within the rendered page: on a collision, fall back to
  the next photo of the album.
- Alternatively (preferred if it fits the layout), render the cover as a **2 × 2 collage** of
  four different photos from the album — four pictures collide far less often than one.
- Albums with fewer photos than the collage needs degrade gracefully to a single image.
- Keep the grid virtualized; the cover choice must stay stable while scrolling (no reshuffle
  when a tile is recycled).

## Where it is
`web/src/pages/AlbumsPage.tsx`, `cover_uid` from `internal/organize`.

## Out of scope
Setting an album cover by hand is a separate write feature and is **not** part of this task.
No data changes — the choice happens at render time.
