# Lower the face-suggestion floor to 25 %, keep bulk confirmation at 40 %

A one-tap suggestion appears from 25 % confidence; "confirm all" keeps today's 40 %.

## Why

On production photo `phfkh30ep2j2qqgol451nu05mv` face 3 is Tomáš Kozák at cosine distance
0.713 = **29 % confidence** — the right person, and the only one on the photo without an
assign button, while four weaker-looking neighbours (54–61 %) each got one. The faces there
are ~2 % of the frame wide, which is exactly the material this library is full of. The user
decided the floor should be 25 %, knowing the band it admits measures worse than the one
above it.

## Requirements

- `SUGGESTION_DISPLAY_FLOOR` moves from 0.4 to **0.25**: a suggestion at ≥ 25 % is a clickable
  chip in `FaceAssignPanel` and a one-tap row offer in the album face-tagging run
  (`useAlbumFaces`).
- Bulk confirmation keeps **0.4** as its own, stricter constant: `bulkConfirmations` — both
  the photo panel's "confirm all" and the album run's batch — offers only faces at ≥ 40 %. One
  click must not write a dozen names from the band measured at ~54 % precision.
- That splits what today's code calls deliberately single ("There is deliberately no second,
  stricter threshold" in `web/src/lib/faceSuggestion.ts`); the comment and its reasoning must
  be rewritten, not left contradicting the code.
- The muted "Nejistý návrh" line keeps its role for everything under 25 %, with no lower
  bound, exactly as now.
- Nothing on the backend changes: `faces.suggestion_max_distance` stays 0.5 and the widening
  fallback in `internal/facematch/faces.go` stays — that is what surfaces a 29 % candidate in
  the first place.
- `review.band_min`/`band_max`/`sure_min` and `cluster.suggestion_max_distance` are untouched.

## Measurement (house rule: a moved threshold arrives with its numbers)

- Re-run the SQL recipe already documented in `docs/THRESHOLDS.md` §"Lowering the floor to
  0.4" against production and report precision + coverage split at **0.25–0.30** and
  **0.30–0.40** (the current table lumps everything under 0.30 together, and 0.25 sits inside
  that lump).
- Record the result, both floors and this photo as the motivating case in
  `docs/THRESHOLDS.md` — the inventory row and a dated section.

- `FacesPanel` / album batch: the confirm-all count ignores a 0.30 face whose row still shows
