# The faces panel lists numbers, not faces

On a group photo of five people the faces panel prints:

```
Obličej #1   [Bez jména]
Obličej #2   [name]
Obličej #3   [name]
Obličej #4   [name]
Obličej #5   [Bez jména]
```

No face previews, only ordinal numbers. Those numbers match small badges next to the boxes
drawn on the photo, but they are **not ordered left to right** — number one was in the
middle, three to its left, five at the far left. Matching "Obličej #4" to a person means
hunting for a tiny numeric badge on the photo.

At the same time the name labels drawn under the boxes overlap each other — with two people
standing next to each other one label lay across another and across a third box. With five
faces it is annoying; on a fifteen-person group photo it is an unreadable pile, and the
library has plenty of those: the query `faces:3 face:new` returns 2 937 photos.

Notably, the **Informace** panel on the same photo shows people as chips **with a small round
face crop** — exactly what the faces panel needs and lacks.

This is the screen where people get named, which is the main way anything is findable later
in a 20 000-photo library. When a row cannot be matched to a face, the work slows down and
the risk of naming the wrong person goes up.

## Requirements
- Put a face crop in every panel row (the `FaceThumb` component already exists and is used in
  `PeoplePanel`). The words "Obličej #N" can then disappear.
- Sort the faces left to right, top to bottom by their box position, so the numbering matches
  the way the eye reads the photo.
- Hovering or focusing a row highlights the matching box on the photo, and hovering a box
  highlights the matching row.
- On the photo, either show a name label only for the hovered/focused box, or collapse
  labels to their number when they would overlap.

## Where it is
`web/src/components/people/FacesPanel.tsx` (rows around lines 74–95),
`web/src/components/people/FaceOverlay.tsx`, `FaceThumb.tsx`.

## Out of scope
No changes to face detection or assignment, no data changes.
