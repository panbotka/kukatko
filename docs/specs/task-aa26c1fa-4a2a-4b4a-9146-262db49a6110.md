# On a phone the faces and info panels cover the photo completely

Open a photo on a 393 × 852 phone and turn on faces: `.kk-viewer__panel.is-open` measures
393 × 852 px with an opaque background plus a 55 % black scrim on top. The photo is still in
the DOM and loaded, but not a single pixel of it is visible. The info panel behaves the same.

That breaks the panel's purpose: the faces panel lists numbered boxes drawn **on the photo**,
so with the photo hidden there is nothing to match the rows against. On a desktop the panel
sits beside the photo in empty black space and works well — the mobile layout is the desktop
one copied without adaptation.

On the same screen the viewer's close cross (top left) overlaps the panel heading — the
letters "In" of "Informace" sit underneath it — and two visually identical round crosses end
up side by side at the same height: the left one closes the photo, the right one closes the
panel. On a family photo that means closing the whole photo by accident.

Related, same area: after a few seconds of inactivity the viewer chrome auto-hides down to
the photo and one small cross. On a desktop any mouse move brings it back; on a phone there
is no such gesture and nothing hints at how to get the controls back — and that cross is the
only way out.

## Requirements
- On narrow viewports the faces and info panels become a **bottom sheet** covering roughly
  40–50 % of the height, leaving the photo visible and pannable above it.
- While a panel is open, the viewer's own close control must not collide with the panel
  heading — move it, or hide it while the panel is open.
- Give the two controls different icons: a back arrow for "leave the photo", a cross for
  "close the panel".
- When the chrome auto-hides, keep the back/close control and the photo title visible (or
  dimmed rather than removed). Everything else may still hide.
- Verify the layout in a real browser at 393 × 852, not only in vitest.

## Where it is
`web/src/components/photo/viewer.css` (`.kk-viewer__panel`, `.kk-viewer__panel-scrim`),
`web/src/components/people/FacesPanel.tsx`, `web/src/components/photo/MetadataPanel.tsx`,
`web/src/hooks/useAutoHideChrome.ts`.

## Out of scope
No data changes.
