# On a phone, 350 of 852 px are chrome — and the timeline is hidden

On a 393 × 852 phone the first photo in the library starts at **y = 350 px**. Above it:
the top bar (49 px), the "Knihovna" heading with the Slideshow and Save view buttons, the
search field, the sort control, the density control plus the Filtry button, the hint
"Filtruje název a popis.", and "Počet fotek: 20637". With the bottom bar (54 px) that leaves
**448 px, i.e. 53 % of the screen**, for photos on the main gallery screen.

At the same time `.kukatko-timeline` is `display: none` on mobile. The timeline — the only
control that can skip a 369 018 px long list to 1965 — does not exist on a phone. All that is
left is scrolling.

Photos are browsed mostly on phones. That is where the app has the least room, spends it on
controls used occasionally, and removes the one tool for moving quickly through a
twenty-thousand-photo library.

## Requirements
- On narrow viewports fold the sort and density controls into the **Filtry** drawer (it has
  plenty of room) and leave only the search field at the top.
- Drop the "Knihovna" heading on mobile — the bottom bar already says where you are.
- Move the photo count next to the filters (see also the drawer footer) and replace the
  "Filtruje název a popis." hint with wording that matches what the field really does.
- Add a **mobile form of the timeline**: either a narrow rail on the right edge, or a floating
  year bubble that appears while scrolling and can be tapped to jump.
- Target: the first photo row starts no lower than roughly 150 px on a 393 × 852 screen.
- Verify in a real browser at 393 × 852 and state the measured value in the commit message.
