# Move photo-detail curation controls into a thumb-reachable strip on mobile

In the immersive photo viewer, all curation controls are crowded at the very top edge
— the hardest place to reach one-handed on a tall phone.

## Current behavior

- `web/src/pages/PhotoDetailPage.tsx` (around lines 714-778, `.kk-viewer__actions`,
  styled in `web/src/components/photo/viewer.css` ~lines 188-195) packs seven controls
  into the top bar — a 5-star RatingStars widget, FlagControl, Favorite, Archive,
  Faces, Edit, Info — plus the truncated title and the fixed close button. On a 360px
  screen these wrap and crowd the top edge; rating/flag/favorite need a two-handed reach.
- What already works (do NOT change): the close button is a persistent ~44px top-left
  target, swipe navigation (`useSwipeNavigation`) and pinch zoom work, and the info
  drawer is full-width with a scrim/✕/Esc and safe-area bottom padding.

## Requirements

- On mobile, relocate the primary curation actions (at least rating, flag, favorite —
  and consider archive) into a bottom action strip within thumb reach, instead of the
  top edge. The strip must respect `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` and not obscure the
  photo (auto-hide with the other chrome if the viewer has an immersive/hide-chrome
  mode, or sit in a translucent bar).
- Keep the close button and prev/next affordances where they are (already reachable).
- Less-used actions (Faces, Edit, Info) may remain in a top/secondary cluster or move
  into the info drawer — your judgment, but the everyday curation actions must be
  thumb-reachable.
- Desktop layout should be preserved (the top action bar is fine with a mouse); scope
  the relocation to small/touch screens.
- Do not regress swipe, pinch-zoom, or the info drawer.

## Testing

- Add/update Vitest tests asserting the curation controls render in the mobile layout
  and remain wired to the same actions.
- `make check` must pass. Update `docs/FRONTEND.md`.
