# Eight pages scroll sideways on a phone

Swept all 29 routes at 393x852 with real touch emulation, comparing
`document.documentElement.scrollWidth` against `clientWidth`. Eight routes overflow
horizontally, so the whole page can be dragged sideways on a phone:

| Route | overflow |
| --- | --- |
| `/system` | 66 px |
| `/account`, `/help` | 12 px |
| `/expand`, `/faces`, `/recognition`, `/outliers`, `/stats` | 9 px |

The remaining 21 routes measure 0.

## Root cause

Measured on `/faces`:

```
.kukatko-main.container   padding-left/right = 0px , width 393, left 0
.row (inside it)          margin-left/right  = -8px
-> the row spans 393 + 16 = 409 px inside a 393 px viewport
```

Bootstrap's `.container` normally carries horizontal padding that absorbs the negative
margins its `.row` children use for gutters. Here that padding is overridden to 0 while the
rows keep their negative margins. On a desktop the container is narrower than the viewport,
so the bleed lands in the spare margin and nothing overflows — which is why this stays
invisible until phone width, where the container *is* the viewport.

On `/system` the effect is also visible as a lost gutter: the "Stav systému" heading and the
left column of cards sit flush against x=0 while the right column runs off-screen.

## Requirements

- No route may overflow horizontally at 393 px wide. Content that is genuinely wide — a
  table, a code block, a diagram — may scroll inside its own container, but the page body
  must not.
- Pages must keep a sensible left and right gutter at phone width; content must not sit
  flush against the screen edge.
- Fix the shared cause rather than patching each of the eight pages. Restoring the
  container's horizontal padding, or removing the rows' negative margins, should clear all
  eight at once.
- Desktop layout must not change. Check a wide viewport before and after.

## Verification

After the change, load each of `/system`, `/account`, `/help`, `/expand`, `/faces`,
`/recognition`, `/outliers` and `/stats` at 393 px wide and confirm
`document.documentElement.scrollWidth === document.documentElement.clientWidth` on every
one, and that the other routes are still 0.

## Testing

Add a style test asserting the container and row gutter declarations stay consistent with
each other, so a future override of one cannot silently reintroduce the overflow.
