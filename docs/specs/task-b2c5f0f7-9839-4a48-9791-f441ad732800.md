# Family network 1/3 — the component walk

Teach `internal/family` to walk the whole reachable network around a subject, reachable as
a third direction, while everything that exists today keeps working.

Read `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-family-network-view-design.md` sections 1 to 4
before starting. This is step 1 of 3; the page is not touched.

## Requirements

- A new walk over the **bipartite** person-and-family graph: a person is joined to a family
  by being one of its partners or one of its children, and both kinds of edge are walked in
  both directions, breadth-first from the root, until nothing new is reached.
- Each member carries a **signed generation**: root 0, a parent −1, a child +1, a partner
  shares the generation of the person they married. Assignment is breadth-first and the
  first write wins, so the nearest relationship names the generation and nobody is reported
  twice.
- A cap on the number of people (400) with the walk keeping the nearest relatives, and a
  `truncated` flag in the result. `MaxDepth = 20` keeps its existing job as a cycle guard
  and is not repurposed.
- A family's `child_uids` are no longer filtered to the walked set for this walk — in a
  component every child of every reached family is itself reached, so the filter is a no-op
  and its removal is what makes aunts and cousins appear.
- Reachable over HTTP as `direction=network` on `GET /subjects/{uid}/tree`. The existing
  `descendants` and `ancestors` directions, the `generations` parameter and the unsigned
  `depth` field all stay exactly as they are — the page must keep working unchanged.
- Tests (integration, against the real test DB): a seeded graph reproducing the production
  shape — a couple with a child, a **parentless sibling group** (both partners NULL, kind
  `unknown`), and the sibling's own child — asserting all five people come back with the
  right signed generations. Plus a cousin-marriage cycle asserting each person appears once
  and the nearest relationship wins, and a graph past the cap asserting `truncated` and
  that the nearest relatives are the ones kept.
- Docs: `docs/API.md` for the new direction and the response shape, `docs/PACKAGES.md` for
  `internal/family` and `internal/familyapi`.

## Implementation notes

- The production shape worth seeding is real: `Ludmila + Aleš → Tomáš`, a parentless
  sibling group holding `Ludmila` and `Dagmar`, and `Dagmar → Petra`. Today's walks reach
  three of those five.
- `internal/photos/store_query.go` has its own recursive SQL for the `family:` search
  filter with its own `familyDepthLimit`. Do not touch it and do not unify them — section 7
  of the design explains why they answer different questions on purpose.
- The existing `UNION`-plus-`MIN(depth)` collapse is the precedent for drawing each person
  once; keep that discipline.
