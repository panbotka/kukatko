# One grid density value is shared by a laptop and a phone

The "Dlaždic na řádek" control stores a single number in `localStorage` under
`kukatko.grid.density`, shared across every device and window width. Set it to 8 on a
desktop, then open the library on a 393 px phone: the grid stays eight columns wide, each
tile is under 50 px, and the favourite heart drawn on the tile ends up larger than the photo
itself — the screen becomes a mesh of white hearts with nothing recognisable underneath.

A fresh phone visit gets the default of 2 and looks fine; the damage happens the moment
someone increases the density once on a computer. Photos are browsed mostly on phones, so
this is a self-inflicted trap with no visible cause for the user.

## Requirements
- Clamp the effective column count by viewport width: at most 3 columns below 576 px and at
  most 4 columns below 768 px, whatever the stored value says.
- The stored preference must not be destroyed by the clamp — going back to a wide window
  restores the user's chosen density.
- The density control must show the value that is actually in effect, and must not offer
  steps that the current viewport will refuse.
- Cover the clamp with unit tests over the density helper (both directions: narrow clamps,
  wide restores).

## Where it is
`web/src/lib/gridDensity.ts` (key `kukatko.grid.density`, around line 79),
`web/src/hooks/useGridDensity.ts`, `web/src/components/library/GridDensityControl.tsx`.

## Out of scope
No data changes. Storing two separate values per breakpoint is an acceptable
implementation, but the clamp is the requirement.
