# Give the upload-link card a side gutter on phones

Found in the v0.30 prerelease walkthrough (staging 54a1504, iPhone 12 emulation). On `/u/:code` the card spans the full viewport width: its rounded corners and border sit on the screen edge with no side gutter, so it looks clipped. Screenshots: `11-u-pick.png` and `13-u-done.png` in the prerelease scratchpad.

## Requirements
- At phone widths (360–430 px) the card has a ~16 px gutter on both sides, or it deliberately goes edge-to-edge without border radius and side borders. Pick one and make it look intentional.
- No horizontal scroll at 360 px. The full-width "Vybrat fotky" / "Nahrát další" buttons keep at least 48 px height.
- Verify visually at 360 and 390 px (pick, uploading, done states). Update `docs/FRONTEND.md` if a layout rule is added.
