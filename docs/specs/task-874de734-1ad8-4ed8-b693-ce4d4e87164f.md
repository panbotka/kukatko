# Person Life Data: Birth/Death Years and Age on Photos

Give subjects optional birth and death years and use them to enrich the person page and photo detail. The library spans 1905–2026, so this turns a person's gallery into a life timeline.

## Requirements
- Subjects of type person get optional integer fields: birth year and death year. Editable by editors in the existing subject edit UI; both clearable. Validation: 1800 ≤ birth ≤ current year, death ≥ birth when both are set.
- The person page header shows the life span when known ("1923–1998", or "*1923" when only birth is set).
- In the person's photo gallery and in the photo-detail face panel, when both the photo's taken date and the person's birth year are known, show the approximate age ("~23 let" / "~23 yrs"). Never show negative or absurd ages — a photo dated before the birth year shows nothing.
- The person page gains decade grouping or a decade jump for the person's photos (navigable by decade of taken date), visually consistent with the existing library timeline styling.
- i18n cs/en. DB migration for the new columns.
- Integration tests (validation, age computation edge cases) and component tests (life span rendering, age label, decade navigation).
