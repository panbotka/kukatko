# The closed photo info panel must not hold keyboard focus

On a photo detail opened without the info panel, tabbing walks into the closed panel: the browser scrolls the photograph 416 px off screen to reveal controls the reader cannot see, and keeps it there for 17 more tab stops.

## Evidence (observed on production, 2026-08-12, 1440 px viewport)

Opening a photo without `?info=1` and pressing Tab repeatedly:

- tab 13 — `scrollLeft 0`, focus on "Dalsi fotka" (correct)
- tab 14 — `scrollLeft 416`, focus on "Zavrit informace" — inside the closed panel
- tabs 15-30 — `scrollLeft 416`, focus walks map +/-, "Zjistit misto", album chips, "Technicke udaje", "Stahnout original"

The panel is `aria-hidden="true"` and `transform: translateX(100%)`, but it is `display: flex; visibility: visible` and **not** `inert`, so its 16 focusable elements stay in the tab order. The ancestor `.kk-viewer` has `scrollWidth 1856 > clientWidth 1440`, so the browser scrolls it to reveal them.

- `web/src/pages/PhotoDetailPage.tsx:1036-1038` — the `<aside>`
- `web/src/components/photo/viewer.css:516-538` — the closed state, `transform: translateX(100%)` with no `visibility` or `inert`

Two defects in one: focusable content inside `aria-hidden` is a WCAG 4.1.2 violation (a screen reader announces nothing while focus sits there), and the visible layout break lands on the most-used screen in the app.

## Requirements

- While the info panel is closed, none of its controls are reachable by Tab, and the photograph never scrolls out of view.
- Opening the panel restores normal focus order inside it; closing it returns focus somewhere sensible rather than nowhere.
- The panel's open/close animation is not broken by the fix.
- The same audit is applied to any other panel that closes by transform on this screen (the faces panel) — fix it too if it has the same defect, and say so.
- Regression test: with the panel closed, assert its controls are not focusable.

## Constraints

- **No mutable operations against production data.** Read-only browsing of production is allowed for verification; writes of any kind are not.
- `make check` must pass. `make check-box` runs the same gate on the build box and is much faster.
