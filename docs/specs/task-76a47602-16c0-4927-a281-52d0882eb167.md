# A crowd should not bury the rest of a photograph's information

The People section of the photo information panel lists every detected face as its own chip.
Measured on the live instance: a concert photograph produced eighteen chips reading
"Nepojmenovaný obličej 1" through "18", which pushed the comments, the technical details and
everything below them far off the panel. Any photograph of an audience behaves this way, and
those faces will realistically never be named.

The instance is live; this task changes presentation only. It must not delete detections,
change assignments, or introduce any new stored state about a face.

## Requirements

- Show a small number of unnamed face chips — about six — and collapse the remainder behind a
  control that says how many are hidden and expands them in place.
- People who already have a name are always shown; the collapsing applies to unnamed faces only.
- Each chip shows a face thumbnail large enough to recognise the person, not a token dot.
- The sections below People (comments, technical details, similar photos) stay reachable without
  a long scroll on a photograph with many faces.
- The collapsed state is the default on every photograph with more unnamed faces than the
  threshold; expanding is per photograph and need not be remembered.

## Explicitly out of scope

An action that dismisses the remaining faces on a photograph as "a crowd, not worth naming" was
considered and is **not** part of this task. There is no subject-independent way to mark a face
as ignored today: face rejections always name the subject they reject ("not this person"), the
`markers.invalid` flag only exists once a face has been named, and the `faces` table has no
ignored state. Adding one means new stored state plus filtering in the candidate search, the
clustering and the review game — a backend change of its own, not a rider on a presentation
fix. Do not start it here.

## Edge cases

- Fewer unnamed faces than the threshold — show them all, with no expand control.
- Every face already named.
- Naming someone while the list is expanded: the list stays coherent without a full reload.
