# Video Player Improvements

Make watching videos comfortable: playback speed, skipping, and scrub previews.

## Requirements
- Playback speed control (0.5×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 2×) in the player UI, remembered for the session.
- Skip buttons ±10 s plus keyboard shortcuts that do NOT conflict with existing photo-detail navigation (arrow keys currently move between photos). Scope video shortcuts to when the player is active/focused (e.g. J/L, or arrows only during playback — decide and document), and update the keyboard-shortcuts help overlay.
- **Scrub previews:** hovering/dragging the timeline shows a small preview thumbnail near the cursor. Generate a storyboard (sprite of frames) per video lazily via the existing job queue and the ffmpeg shell-out, stored like other derived media. Storyboard generation is idempotent and enqueued on demand (e.g. on first playback) — never eagerly for the whole library. Videos without a storyboard degrade gracefully: no preview, no errors.
- Mobile: speed and skip controls must be usable on touch; the scrub preview may be desktop-only if the touch UX would suffer — decide and document.
- i18n cs/en for new controls and help entries.
- Tests: Go tests for the storyboard job and its endpoint (including "not generated yet"); frontend tests for the controls and shortcut scoping.
- Update docs/API.md, docs/PACKAGES.md, and docs/FRONTEND.md.
