# /stats talks about "Embeddingy" and offers no action

The statistics page — visible to **every** user, not just admins — has five cards. One of
them is titled **"EMBEDDINGY"** and its rows read "Embeddingů celkem 20 664", "Fotek
s embeddingem 20 664", "Fotek bez embeddingu 242". The "Lidé a zvířata" card highlights
"Nepojmenovaných obličejů **16 585**" in yellow — and links nowhere. There is not a single
link into the app on the whole page.

"Embedding" has no business in an interface used by a family. And a yellow "16 585 unnamed
faces" is effectively a call to action with no action behind it.

## Requirements
- Rename the card and its rows to plain language, e.g. "Vyhledávání podle obsahu" with rows
  like "Připraveno k hledání podle obsahu 20 664 fotek · Zbývá zpracovat 242". Do the same
  pass over any other jargon on the page.
- Turn the highlighted numbers into links: unnamed faces → `/review`, photos without a face →
  the library with `faces:0`, trash → `/trash`.
- Do not show a link the current role cannot follow (a viewer must not be sent to `/review`).
- Czech and English translations.

## Where it is
`web/src/pages/StatsPage.tsx`, `web/src/components/LibraryStatsCards.tsx`,
texts in `web/src/i18n`.

## Out of scope
No changes to how the numbers are computed, no data changes.
