## Goal
When someone tags me (my linked subject, see `internal/personme`) in library photos, I get notified over the channels I enabled for kind `tagged` (in-app / push / e-mail). The notification is **merged per album**, as the user decided:
- **one notification per album** that contains newly tagged photos of me in the closing window: title like "Byl(a) jsi označen(a) na 5 fotkách v albu Pouť 2026", link → the album filtered to me (or the notification page with its frozen photo set);
- photos in **no album** go into one summary notification "… na N dalších fotkách";
- a photo in **several albums counts in each** of them.

## Current state
- `internal/tagnotifyjob`: the `recorder.go` TagObserver writes `tag_notices(user_uid, photo_uid)` in the tagging transaction and opens a window of `push.tags.window` (default 1h). The `tag_notify` job closes it in `service.go:Close`: it re-checks visibility and markers, orders newest first, caps at 5000, then makes **one** notification. It knows nothing about albums.
- Today the recorder and the enqueuer are gated by `push.enabled`. After 1/4 that gating must move to "any channel for `tagged` is wanted and possible". Recording must also work with push off, as long as in-app or e-mail is wanted.

## What to build
1. In `Close`, group the surviving photos by album (`album_photos`). The album has to be visible to the recipient: a private album only for a curator+, matching the rules elsewhere. Albums the recipient cannot see fall into "other photos".
2. Produce one notification per group through `notification.Deliver`, with the frozen photo set per group. Use a deterministic order: albums by the newest tagged photo, "other" last.
3. **Cap the push/mail fan-out**: with many albums, one window could spam. Above N groups (e.g. 5), collapse the remainder into the "other" summary. Make N a constant or a config key (if a config key, then also `config.example.yaml` + `docs/OPERATIONS.md`). Document the choice.
4. Rename the config key `push.tags.window` → something channel-neutral (e.g. `notifications.tags.window`), keep the old key as a deprecated alias, and update `docs/OPERATIONS.md`.
5. Untagging before the window closes still un-announces (existing behaviour, keep the test).
6. E-mail: one mail per window listing the albums with counts, not one mail per album.
7. Tests:

   - integration test of `Close` with photos in 0, 1 and 2 albums plus a private album, against the test DB.
