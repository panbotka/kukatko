# Subject page: drop the outlier review section, keep a link

Replace the "Je to pořád tato osoba?" section on a person's page with a single link to the
`/outliers` page, and delete the section.

## Background

`web/src/components/people/Outliers.tsx`, mounted for curators at `SubjectPage.tsx:460`, is a
poorer copy of the `/outliers` page:

- each suspicious face is a `<Link>` to the photo wrapped around a fixed-size `FaceCrop` — a link
  where a button belongs, with no way to enlarge it and no context kept around the face box;
- the verdict is one `variant="link"` button ("Není to tato osoba", `.kk-outlier-answer`) — a thin
  link rather than a real button — and the other half of the answer ("yes, still them") is missing.

`OutliersPage` does the same job properly through `OutlierCard`: a context crop, an enlarge button
with the review lightbox, both verdicts, selection and bulk unassign, keyboard shortcuts, grid
density, the distance threshold and the stats. It is reachable from the navigation.

The one thing the section does that the page cannot is tell a curator, while they are looking at a
person, that this person has suspicious faces at all — `/outliers` only summarises the subject you
already picked. That signal is kept; everything else goes.

## Requirements

- Delete `web/src/components/people/Outliers.tsx` and `web/src/components/people/Outliers.test.tsx`.
- In place of the mount at `SubjectPage.tsx:460`, render a single link to `/outliers?subject={uid}`
  naming how many faces are on offer. The count comes from a `fetchOutliers` read for this subject,
  as the section made today.
- Render it only for a curator and only when there is at least one suspicious face — exactly the
  rule the section followed: nothing at all when there is nothing to ask about, no titled empty box.
- Nothing renders when the read fails or reports "not meaningful" either. Reviewing outliers is not
  what a person's page is for, and the page it links to reports its own failures.
- It must be a real link: keyboard reachable, with an accessible name that includes the count. Not a
  bare icon.
- Remove the `.kk-outlier-answer` rules from `web/src/components/people/outliers.css` (around lines
  64–78). The rest of that file (`.kk-face-marker`) belongs to `OutlierCard` and
  `DuplicateMarkerCrop` and stays.
- Remove the whole `outliers.*` block from the cs and en locale files — 13 keys, used by nothing
  else, since the page runs on `outliersPage.*` — and add the one key the new link needs.
- `FaceCrop` (seven other consumers) and `fetchOutliers` (still called by the page) must survive.

## Testing

- `web/src/pages/SubjectPage.test.tsx`: keep the `fetchOutliers` mock. Assert the link appears with
  its count when the read returns faces, and that nothing renders when it returns none, reports not
  meaningful, or fails. A viewer without the curate right sees no link.
- `web/src/pages/OutliersPage.test.tsx` stays untouched and must still pass — it becomes the only
  coverage of outlier review.

## Docs

- `docs/FRONTEND.md`: rewrite the SubjectPage description (~3727); ~5073 and ~5083, where the
  section is cited as the model for a quiet control rather than a row of red `outline-danger`
  buttons — that argument now has to point at `OutlierCard` or go; ~5112 (`.kk-outlier-answer`); and
  the `Outliers` mentions at ~3016 and ~4942. The `fetchOutliers` mention at ~6820 stays.
- `CLAUDE.md` needs no change: frontend only, no package added or removed.
