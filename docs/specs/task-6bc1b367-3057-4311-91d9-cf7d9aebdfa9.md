The "refuse during a move" rule covers only two repairs.
- D4 (small): an on-demand thumbnail rebuild hit by the move answers 422 instead of a retryable status; the Finish sweep and the root-orphan repair skip the "another project still at root" check; while the move's job is dead-lettered, thumbnail/HLS jobs re-defer every minute (undocumented).
