# Review game: a face crop beside the question

Seen on the box staging (commit 68e805e, 2026-09-06). A review question "Je na fotce Testovací osoba 6?" was asked over a group photograph of about thirty people; on a phone the whole landscape frame is 393 px wide and the highlighted face is roughly 20 px — impossible to judge without opening the photo. Desktop is better but the same problem exists for crowd shots. Screenshots: `/mnt/nas-botka/kukatko-stage-test/m-review.png` (phone) and `r-review.png` (desktop).

## What already exists

- The backend serves a face as its own small square: `GET /api/v1/photos/{uid}/face?box=x,y,w,h` (`internal/photoapi/http.go` ≈ line 359, `handleFaceCrop`, backed by `internal/avatar`), and a person's avatar comes from `GET /subjects/{uid}/avatar` (`internal/avatarapi`). The review queue already carries the face box it highlights.
- The page is `web/src/pages/ReviewPage.tsx`; it has the "open the photo" control in the corner of the image and the swipe/keyboard answering.

## Requirements

- Above or beside the question, show two squares side by side: the judged face (the crop of the highlighted box) and the person being asked about (their avatar), each about 96–128 px, labelled so it reads as "this face — this person?". On the phone they sit between the question and the photo; on desktop next to the question.
- The full photo stays below with the highlighted box as today; tapping/clicking the face crop zooms the photo view onto the face (reuse the existing zoom or scroll-into-view behaviour if the page has one; otherwise a simple enlarged crop overlay is enough).
- Label questions (source `labels`) are unaffected — the crop row is only for face questions.
- Keyboard and swipe answering keep working; the crop row must not steal the swipe gesture area.
- Tests: Vitest for a face question rendering the two images with the right URLs and the label question rendering none; a test that the crop tap/zoom control is reachable.
- Docs: `docs/FRONTEND.md` (ReviewPage) and `README.md` if the review game section describes the screen.

## Out of scope

- No backend change; if the queue payload lacks the subject uid or face box for some question kind, note it in the commit and skip the crop for that kind rather than extending the API in this task.
