# Merge "Provoz" and "Správa" into one section in the user menu

The top bar carries two separate admin dropdowns — `OPERATIONS_GROUP` ("Provoz", maintainer:
Import, Maintenance, System) and `GOVERNANCE_GROUP` ("Správa", admin: Users, Audit). They eat
horizontal space and belong to the signed-in identity, not to browsing the library. Fold them
into a single **"Správa"** section inside the user-name dropdown.

## Requirements

- Both groups disappear from the top navigation bar.
- The user dropdown gains a **"Správa" section** — a labelled group with a divider, not a nested
  submenu — placed **between the account block** (Account / My photos / Stats / Help) **and the
  Log out item**.
- Item order: Import, Maintenance, System, Users, Audit. Icons stay as they are today.
- **Per-item role gating is preserved, not applied to the section as a whole**: `/import`,
  `/maintenance` and `/system` need maintainer; `/users` and `/audit` need admin. An admin who is
  not a maintainer sees only Users and Audit. Someone with neither role sees no section at all —
  no orphan heading, no stray divider.
- The **mobile drawer** (`MobileNavDrawer`) gets the same merged structure, so the two widths do
  not teach two different menus.
- Keep the keyboard-shortcut help in sync (`lib/shortcuts.ts`, `KeyboardShortcutsHelp`,
  `HelpPage`) wherever it refers to these groups.
- i18n cs + en: the section reuses `nav.admin`. Remove the keys left unused by dropping the
  "Provoz" group (`nav.operations`, `nav.titles.operations`) rather than leaving orphans.
- Tests: update `Layout.test.tsx` and `MobileNavDrawer.test.tsx` — the section renders in full
  for a maintainer, as two items for an admin, and not at all for an editor/viewer; assert it
  sits before Log out.
- Verify in a real browser at 1280 px and 1400 px widths: the bar must get measurably shorter.
  Note the known pre-existing defect — for a maintainer the inline bar already overflows its
  container below 1400 px — which this change should resolve.

## Out of scope

- Changing which roles may reach which pages.
- Renaming routes.
- Touching the "Procházet" (Browse) and "Nástroje" (Tools) groups.
