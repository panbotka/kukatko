# First-run welcome

Somebody signing in for the first time gets no orientation, and the link between an
account and the person in the photographs is buried in the account screen. Show a welcome,
once.

What already exists: `GET /auth/me` reports whether this account has seen the welcome and
which subject it is linked to; `POST /auth/welcome-seen` records that it has seen it;
`GET /settings/welcome` returns the administrator's Markdown text; and `PUT /auth/subject`
links the signed-in account to a subject of the library.

## Requirements

- After signing in, an account that has never seen the welcome gets a modal over the
  library. It appears once; the rest of the interface stays where it is behind it.
- **Step one** renders the administrator's Markdown text. Render it with `react-markdown`
  together with `rehype-sanitize` — add both dependencies if they are not already present.
  Links open in a new tab with the usual protections. When the administrator has left the
  text empty, skip step one entirely and open on step two.
- **Step two** asks who the reader is: a search field over the library's people, showing
  each candidate's name and face. Choosing one and confirming links the account to that
  person. Nothing is linked until the reader confirms.
- Both steps can be skipped, and the modal can be closed. However it ends, the account is
  recorded as having seen it and it does not come back.
- An account already linked to a person skips step two: it shows who the account is linked
  to and offers to change it, rather than asking a question that is already answered.
- Failing to record the visit must not trap the reader — the modal still closes and the
  library stays usable.
- The modal is usable on a phone: full width, the search field reachable above the
  keyboard, no horizontal scrolling.
- All text goes through i18n with Czech and English strings.

## Implementation notes

- An open modal is a portal. Query it in tests by its dialog role and scope assertions
  inside it; its siblings are not hidden from the accessibility tree.

## Definition of done

- Component tests cover: the modal appearing for a first-time account, not appearing for
  an account that has seen it, an empty welcome text skipping step one, choosing a person,
  skipping, and an account that is already linked.
- `docs/FRONTEND.md` describes the new component.
- `make check` passes.
