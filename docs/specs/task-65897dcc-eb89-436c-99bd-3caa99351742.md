# Album face-tagging page: work through an album photo by photo

After an event the whole album is uploaded at once and every face has to be named. Give the
album a dedicated full-screen mode that walks the photos one by one and offers the most likely
person for each face, so the normal rhythm is yes, yes, yes.

## Entry point

- Add an "Obličeje" / "Faces" button to the album header on `AlbumDetailPage`
  (`web/src/pages/AlbumDetailPage.tsx`), in the `HeaderActions` `secondary` slot, gated on
  `canWrite`.
- The button carries the number of photos in the album that still have an unassigned face, and
  is hidden when that number is zero. The count comes from the existing photo search —
  `GET /photos?album=<uid>&q=face:new&limit=1` and its `total`.

## The page

- New route `/albums/:uid/faces`, full screen outside `Layout` like `/review`, `RequireRole
  role="editor"`, with an explicit close that returns to the album.
- The queue is the photos of that album that have at least one unassigned face, in the album's
  own order. Nothing else is in scope — no whole-library mode, no other filters.
- One photo at a time: progress ("Fotka 12/86"), the photo with numbered face boxes, and below
  it one row per unassigned face — crop, number, the top suggested person with its percentage,
  a confirm and a dismiss control. Hovering a row highlights its box, as in the faces panel.
- **Confirm** assigns the face to that suggestion. **Dismiss** only removes the row for this
  visit — it writes nothing, records no rejection, and the face may be offered again next time.
  (This deliberately differs from the review game, where "no" is a stored rejection.)
- **"Potvrdit vše (N)"** confirms every face on the photo whose top suggestion is offered.
  If two faces top-suggest the same subject, confirm only the stronger one and leave the other —
  never two markers for one person on one photo.
- Faces with no offered suggestion are listed but not actionable; they stay for the photo detail.
- The page advances to the next photo automatically once every face on the current one is
  confirmed or dismissed, and "Přeskočit" advances immediately. Going back to the previous photo
  is possible.
- Photos whose faces turn out to have no offered suggestion are skipped without being shown.
- Keyboard: digits confirm the n-th face, one key confirms all, one advances, one goes back,
  Esc closes. Fully usable without a mouse; i18n cs + en.

## Threshold

- Lower the suggestion display floor from 0.5 to **0.4 confidence** in
  `web/src/lib/faceSuggestion.ts` (`SUGGESTION_DISPLAY_FLOOR`). This is a global change: the
  photo detail's faces panel starts offering 40 % suggestions as one-tap buttons too, which is
  intended. Record the new value and the reason in `docs/THRESHOLDS.md` next to the existing
  rationale for 0.5. Existing tests that pin the old floor have to be updated.

## Constraints

- **No new Go package and no new endpoint.** Reuse `GET /photos` (album scope + `q=face:new`),
  `GET /photos/{uid}/faces` fetched per photo just in time with the next photos prefetched, and
  `assignFace()` → `POST /photos/{uid}/faces/assign` for every confirmation. Reuse
  `rankSuggestions()` and the existing assign-request builder rather than writing new ones.
- A failed confirmation leaves the face unassigned and reports the failure; the run continues.

## Tests and docs

- Page and hook tests: queue built from the album, photo skipped when nothing is actionable,
  confirm one, confirm all, the same-subject rule, dismiss writes nothing, auto-advance, going
  back, failure handling, the album button's count and its hidden state.
- `docs/FRONTEND.md` — the new page and the album header button; `docs/THRESHOLDS.md` — the new
