# Command palette: collections before individual photos

The command palette (`/` or Cmd-K, `web/src/components/search/SearchCommand.tsx`) lists
its result groups photos first, then people, then albums, then labels. Somebody searching
for "Veselice" almost always wants the album or the label, not one of the two hundred
photographs that match it. An individual photo is the least specific answer and belongs
last.

## Requirements

- The result groups are ordered: **albums, then labels, then people, then photos.**
- Everything above the groups keeps its position: a direct UID hit stays at the very top,
  and the "search everything" action keeps its place ahead of the result groups.
- Keyboard navigation follows the new visual order — arrowing down walks albums, labels,
  people and photos in that order, and the first highlighted row is the first row on
  screen. No group may be reachable in an order different from how it is drawn.
- Groups with no results stay absent, as they are today. The order of whichever groups do
  appear follows the rule above.
- Nothing about the backend response changes; this is presentation only.

## Definition of done

- The palette's existing tests are updated to assert the new order, and a test covers
  arrowing from the first row through to a photo row.
- `docs/FRONTEND.md` is updated where it describes the palette's groups.
- `make check` passes.
