# Dialogs other than fullscreen modals ignore the safe area

Every destructive confirm, both phone filter drawers, the viewer's side drawer and the slideshow
settings panel put a control in a band iOS owns.

## Background

Commit `5e12de3` insets `.modal-fullscreen` and `.modal-fullscreen-sm-down`
(`web/src/styles/app.css:139-155`, guarded by `styles/safeArea.test.ts`). Everything else is still
open; `.modal` at `app.css:122` only re-declares a z-index. Four independently reachable places:

1. **Every non-fullscreen `scrollable` modal.** `.modal-dialog-scrollable` is
   `height: calc(100% - var(--bs-modal-margin) * 2)` and `--bs-modal-margin` is `0.5rem` below `sm`,
   so even a short `scrollable` dialog is forced to full viewport height with its top edge at y≈8px;
   the header's padding puts `.btn-close` around y=24–48px, inside the 47–59px inset. `scrollable`
   appears in 23 files, and `components/ConfirmModal.tsx:68` is `centered scrollable` — that is every
   destructive confirm in the app. Also `components/KeyboardShortcutsHelp.tsx:94`,
   `components/users/ResetLinkModal.tsx:118`, `components/account/PhotoPickerModal.tsx:82`,
   `components/people/MoveFacesModal.tsx:124`, `components/people/MergeSubjectModal.tsx:85`,
   `pages/UsersPage.tsx:615`. Their footers sit 8px off a 34px home indicator too.
2. **The phone filter drawers.** `components/library/FilterBar.tsx:516` and
   `components/map/MapFilterBar.tsx:170` render a bare `Offcanvas placement="end"` with a
   `closeButton` header and no class, so the `.kk-navdrawer` insets (`app.css:961-963`) never reach
   them. That ✕ is the only way out other than the backdrop. Their footers are already correct
   (`.kukatko-filter-footer`, `app.css:1854`).
3. **The viewer's side-drawer head at ≥768px** (`components/photo/viewer.css:854`; the drawer at
   `:813` touches top and right). That branch also serves a landscape iPhone Pro Max and every iPad.
   Its ✕ is at `pages/PhotoDetailPage.tsx:1676`. The phone bottom-sheet branch below 768px is
   already correct.
4. **The slideshow settings panel** (`components/slideshow/slideshow.css:236` and `:244`).
   `bottom: 4.5rem` reserves 72px for `.slideshow__controls`, but that sibling correctly adds the
   bottom inset (`slideshow.css:182`) and grows past it; the panel has no z-index of its own and is
   the earlier sibling, so the bar paints over its last rows and takes their taps. Its
   `max-height: calc(100vh - 8rem)` is the one `100vh` case in the app: under `viewport-fit=cover`
   `100vh` is the whole screen, so the top edge lands around 56px, under a 59px Dynamic Island.

## Requirements

- For the modals, one rule rather than a patch per dialog. Insetting `.modal` itself works because it
  is `position: fixed; inset: 0`: the margin box moves inward and the scrollable height then resolves
  against the padded box, so the dialog shrinks instead of overflowing. A top margin alone would
  leave it too tall by the inset. Every current and future modal inherits it.
- A fullscreen modal must end up inset exactly once — the new rule must not double up with the
  existing fullscreen one.
- The two filter drawers get the insets the nav drawer has (top, right, bottom; left deliberately
  omitted there). A shared class or widening the existing rule are both fine; do not paste padding
  into each component.
- Add the insets to the viewer side-drawer head **inside the ≥768px branch only**. The phone sheet
  has no top or right screen edge and must not gain padding for one — that is the dead-padding
  mistake.
- Slideshow settings: add the bottom inset to its offset so the controls bar cannot overlap it, and
  subtract both insets from its `max-height` so the top edge stays clear. Fixing the bottom without
  the height makes the top worse.

## Testing

- Extend `web/src/styles/safeArea.test.ts`: the modal rule names all four insets; a fullscreen modal
  is not inset twice; both filter drawers resolve to the inset rule; the viewer head rule is inside
  the ≥768px branch and the sheet branch carries no top/right inset; the slideshow panel's offset and
  max-height both carry insets.
- Component tests for `ConfirmModal`, both filter bars, the viewer and the slideshow pass untouched —
  this is CSS plus at most a class name.
- `env()` is zero in desktop Chromium, so measure on a standalone harness built from the real
  stylesheets with literal iPhone insets written in, the way `5e12de3` did. Report the close button's
  rect for `ConfirmModal` and for a filter drawer, portrait and landscape, before and after. Whether
  it works on the user's iPhone is theirs to confirm, not yours to claim.
