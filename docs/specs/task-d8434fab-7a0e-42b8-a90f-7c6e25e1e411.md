# Curator role 4/5 — frontend foundation

Teach the frontend the new role: the ladder, the guards, the routes, the admin select,
the navigation and the translations. After this task the role is assignable and lands in
the right places; individual controls on pages are unlocked by step 5.

Read `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-curator-role-design.md` section 7 before starting.
Requires steps 1 to 3, so that every route a curator reaches already accepts it.

## Requirements

- `web/src/services/auth.ts`: `curator` in the `Role` union and in `ROLE_RANK` between
  viewer and editor, plus a `canCurate` helper beside `canWrite`.
- `web/src/auth/AuthContext.tsx` and `AuthProvider.tsx` expose `canCurate`.
- `web/src/pages/ForbiddenPage.tsx`: the exhaustive `MESSAGE_KEYS` map gets the curator's
  sentence. The build fails until it does — that is the compiler pointing at the work.
- `web/src/App.tsx` route gates become `role="curator"` for `/review`,
  `/albums/:uid/faces`, `/people/clusters`, `/faces`, `/expand`, `/recognition`,
  `/outliers`, `/duplicate-markers` and `/upload`. They stay `role="editor"` for
  `/duplicates`, `/duplicates/compare` and `/trash`.
- `web/src/services/users.ts`: `curator` into `ROLES` in ladder order, so the admin role
  select offers it. It stays assignable by any admin — only `maintainer` is restricted to
  maintainers, and that must not change.
- Navigation — `components/navItems.ts`, `Layout.tsx`, `MobileNavDrawer.tsx`,
  `MobileTabBar.tsx`: the curation entries follow `canCurate`, everything else keeps
  `canWrite`.
- i18n in both `web/src/i18n/locales/cs/common.json` and `en/common.json`:
  `roles.curator` (Czech label *kurátor*), `forbidden.message.curator`, and the role's
  paragraph in `help.sections.roles`.
- Do not change page-level or panel-level `canWrite` checks yet. A curator seeing fewer
  buttons than it is entitled to is the safe intermediate state; the reverse is not.
- Tests: vitest over the gating helpers, the route guards and the navigation, following
  the existing `canWrite` tests.
- Docs: `docs/FRONTEND.md` for `canCurate`, the route gates and the role select.

## Implementation notes

- `GuardRole = Exclude<Role, 'viewer'>` picks the new role up on its own, which is what
  makes `ForbiddenPage` fail the build. Let it.
- Known pre-existing failure: `web/src/pages/ReviewPage.test.tsx`, case "leaves no
  snapshot behind a finished round", fails on main under the full vitest suite and passes
  in isolation. If `make check` is red only there, it is not this task's regression.
