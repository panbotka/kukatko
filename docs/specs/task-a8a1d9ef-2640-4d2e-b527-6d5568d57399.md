# Footer job-status chips are cryptic

UI/UX review finding #3 (2026-10-11). The page footer ("Provozuje SDH Veselice · GitHub") shows tiny 11px badges on the right for maintainers: a red "trvale se nepovedlo 17" and sometimes a teal "ve frontě 1". They have no icon, no explanation, no link, and sit next to the credits line where nobody expects an operational alert.

## Requirements
- Replace the bare chips with a readable status line for the same audience (maintainers only, as today): an icon plus a full phrase, e.g. "17 úloh trvale selhalo" / "1 úloha ve frontě", with a Czech-correct plural and an English translation.
- The status line links to the place where the maintainer can act (the jobs/system page that lists failed jobs).
- Font size at least 12px; the failure state keeps the danger colour (via palette tokens), the queued state is neutral/muted.
- When there is nothing to report, nothing is rendered (as today).
- Keep the credits line as it is; the status may move to its own row on narrow viewports instead of squeezing the credits.
- Update the footer's tests for the new text and link.
