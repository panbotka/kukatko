## Goal (user request)
The map page (`/map`) already exists: clustered markers over mapy.com tiles, filters in the URL, a coverage sentence. The user wants it **substantially improved, "in the style of Apple Photos"**. Make the map a way to **browse photos by place**, not a pin chart.

## Reference: what Apple Photos' map does (the target feel)
- **Clusters are photos, not circles.** Each cluster shows a rounded-square **thumbnail of a representative photo** (the newest or best), with a white border, a soft shadow and a small **count badge** in the corner. A single photo is the same tile without a badge.
- **Zooming** splits clusters smoothly into smaller groups and finally into individual photo tiles. Tiles don't overlap into a mess (the cluster radius is tuned to the tile size).
- **Tapping a cluster** zooms to its extent. If it can't split further (the same spot), it opens the **photos of that cluster as a grid**, and a photo then opens in the viewer.
- **"Photos in this area" grid:** a panel (desktop: a side panel; phone: a bottom sheet with peek / half / full positions) lists the photos inside the **current viewport**, newest first, and updates as you pan or zoom (debounced). It is a normal library grid: tap → viewer, with prev/next through the area's photos.
- Opening the map starts at the extent of all geotagged photos (or the URL's viewport). A photo's detail/info panel "show on map" lands here zoomed to it with the photo highlighted.
- Calm, photo-first chrome: the map fills the screen and the controls are minimal (the mapset switch as a small floating control).

## What to build
1. **Cluster and marker icons as photo tiles:**
   - the representative photo = the newest in the cluster (or pick a better rule and document it);
   - count badge; blurhash placeholder while the image loads;
   - the right thumb size for the tile's CSS size × DPR;
   - a selected/hovered state;
   - accessible: each tile is a focusable button whose label is "N fotek, <place/date range>".
2. **Feed shape:** check whether the GeoJSON feed carries what the tiles need (uid, thumb URL, blurhash, taken_at). If not, extend it cheaply. Mind the payload size for the whole library: maybe a compact array format, and maybe thumbs minted lazily for the visible tiles only. Measure it against prod-sized data (thousands of points) and record the numbers in `docs/PERF.md`.
3. **Cluster tap behaviour:** zoom to bounds; at max zoom / same coordinates, open the cluster's photos in the grid panel.
4. **Area grid panel:**
   - desktop: a right side panel, collapsible;
   - phone: a bottom sheet with peek (count + a strip), half and full positions, draggable, touch-friendly, without the page itself scrolling sideways;
   - the content is the photos in the viewport bounds, newest first, virtualized, with an "N fotek v této oblasti" header;
   - viewer navigation within that set;
   - the URL keeps the viewport and the open/closed panel state ("Back always works").
5. **Polish:**
   - smooth zoom animation;
   - the initial fit to all photos;
   - "show on map" from the photo detail deep-links with the photo highlighted;
   - the existing coverage sentence and filters (dates, archive, mapset) are kept but visually quieter.
   Colours only from palette tokens; icons via `Icon`; i18n cs + en.
6. **Performance:** the map must stay fluid with the full prod library on a mid-range phone. Use `chunkedLoading`, and make sure the photo-tile icons don't load every thumbnail at once (only visible clusters, lazy `img`). Write down what you measured.
