# Photo detail: stop the drawer opening itself

The faces overlay and the metadata ("Informace") view open unasked. Drop the persisted
faces preference and make the drawer show only the view its URL names.

## Background

Three separate mechanisms open the viewer's drawer without being asked:

1. `web/src/lib/faceOverlayPref.ts` keeps "faces on" in localStorage forever, and an effect in
   `PhotoDetailPage.tsx` then stamps `panel=faces` into the URL on every mount where that
   preference is set — including a photo opened from the grid, from a notification or from an
   external link.
2. `showInfo` is derived as "neither faces nor edits", so a URL naming `panel=faces` on a photo
   whose faces cannot be shown falls through to the metadata view: the drawer stays open on a
   view nobody asked for.
3. `facesAvailable` includes `!loadingNext`, so while a neighbour loads the faces view collapses
   and the drawer flashes the metadata view, then flips back.

Paging already carries the open panel in the URL (`neighborTo`), independently of localStorage —
browsing an album with the faces up keeps them up with no stored preference at all.

## Requirements

- Delete `web/src/lib/faceOverlayPref.ts` and every use of it: `facesPreferredRef`, the auto-open
  effect, the preference writes in the faces toggle. Nothing may read or write
  `kukatko.faces.overlay` any more.
- Faces come up only on an explicit action (the toggle button, the `m` key) or because the URL
  names `panel=faces`.
- A photo opened with no `panel` in its URL — from the grid, a notification deeplink, an external
  link — shows the plain photo: drawer shut, no boxes.
- Stepping to a neighbour keeps the open view exactly as it does today (the panel travels in the
  URL); so do reload and Back/Forward.
- The drawer shows the view its URL names and never substitutes another: the metadata view appears
  only for `panel=info` (or the legacy `info=1`).
- A named view that cannot be shown on this photo at all shuts the drawer instead of falling
  through to the metadata: `panel=edits` without write rights, `panel=faces` or `panel=edits` on a
  video or live photo.
- Keep the faces view up while `panel=faces` on a still without a crop even when no faces are
  loaded yet — the panel shows its own loading state while a neighbour loads, and an honest empty
  count on a photo that has none. No metadata flash between photos.
- Drawing the boxes over the image keeps its current guards (neighbour still loading, a saved or
  previewed crop, a video). Only the drawer's *view* is decoupled from them.

## Out of scope

- Do NOT start ignoring a `panel=` that is already in the URL on direct entry. It would break
  "a reload brings back what I was reading" and the audit log's own `panel=faces` links; once the
  preference is gone, `panel=faces` reaches a URL only because someone opened it on that photo.

## Testing

- The four localStorage assertions in `web/src/pages/PhotoDetailPage.test.tsx` (around lines 2021,
  2039, 2125, 2129) describe the removed behaviour — rewrite them.
- New coverage: grid entry leaves the drawer shut; `panel=faces` survives paging; a photo without
  faces keeps the faces view instead of showing the metadata; `panel=edits` as a viewer shuts the
  drawer; no metadata view renders while a neighbour loads with faces open.
