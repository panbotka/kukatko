# 113 labels as a single column of full-width rows, with no controls

`/labels` prints 113 labels as one column of full-width rows — each row spans the whole
1 106 px to carry one word and a number, and the document ends up 5 730 px tall. The page has
no controls at all: a heading and 113 links.

They are sorted alphabetically, so the beginning looks like this:

```
Dolňák 36 · Dum11 8 · Dum12 5 · Dum20 3 · Dum32 2 · Dum33 3 · Dum38 7 · Dum4 7 ·
Dum41 4 · Dum47 9 · Dum51 5 · Dum56 3 · Dum59 7 · Dum68 6 …
```

Dozens of `DumNN` labels (house numbers) occupy the whole start of the list and push the
meaningful labels off the first screens. On a phone 14 rows fit per screen, so it takes
several screens of "Dum" before the list gets past the letter D.

## Requirements
- Render labels as a wrapping cloud of pills/chips instead of a column of full-width rows, so
  113 labels fit into one or two screens.
- Add a search field over label names.
- Add sorting: by photo count (default) / alphabetically.
- Optionally group numeric-pattern labels (`Dum\d+`) under one expandable entry so they do
  not flood the list.
- Keep the view state (search, sort) in the URL like the rest of the app.

## Where it is
`web/src/pages/LabelsPage.tsx`.

## Out of scope
No renaming or merging of labels, no data changes.
